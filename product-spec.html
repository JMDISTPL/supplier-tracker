Sourcing Notes

  • • Wood: Acacia, kiln-dried, no knots/cracks. Walnut stain + food-safe seal.
  • • Routing: One continuous cracker groove per end. Two serving wells on right end. Three bowl recesses on left end.
  • • Magnets: 8 N52 neodymium. 2 per connection edge. Drilled + sealed.
  • • Bowls: Standard square white ceramic. Any standard 2.5-3" bowl works. No custom color.
  • • Knives: Basic 4-piece cheese knife set. Any variety pack. No custom order needed.
  • • Box: Full-color printed lid, plain base. Foam insert with cutouts for all pieces.
  • • Sample rounds: 1st sample = board + magnet fit only. 2nd sample = final product + packaging.
